# Licensing

settle is released under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE).
In plain terms: **free for people, paid for companies.**

This page is a readable summary and is not itself the licence. Where the two
disagree, [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) governs.

## Free, no permission needed

You can use, modify, fork and redistribute settle at no cost, for any
noncommercial purpose. That includes:

- personal study, hobby projects and experiments
- academic research and teaching, at any institution
- evaluating settle to decide whether you want a commercial licence
- charities, nonprofits, public research bodies and government institutions,
  regardless of how they are funded
- writing about it, quoting it, and building noncommercial things on top of it

Reading the source, running the benchmark and reviewing the code is always free
and always fine, including when you are evaluating it on a company's behalf.

## Needs a commercial licence

You need a paid licence if settle is used in or for a business. Concretely:

- reconciling real invoices or payments for a company, including your own
- any use inside a for-profit company's operations or internal tooling
- building settle into a product or service you sell, host or resell
- consulting or client work that uses settle to deliver the engagement

The dividing line is the *purpose*, not the amount of code you use or whether
you modified it. One person running settle over their employer's bank statement
is commercial use, even though it is one person.

## Getting a commercial licence

Email **nagytmas@gmail.com** with a sentence about how you want to use it.
Terms are straightforward and negotiable — per-company, perpetual or annual,
with or without support. Startups and small teams get sensible pricing; ask.

I am also happy to grant a free exception for a case that falls on the wrong
side of the line but is clearly in the spirit of the noncommercial grant. Ask
rather than assume.

## Why not MIT

MIT permits unlimited commercial use. That is the right choice for a library
meant to be adopted everywhere, and the wrong choice here: reconciliation is a
problem companies pay for, and this engine solves it well enough that giving it
away to them for free would be the only irreversible decision in the project.
PolyForm Noncommercial keeps the code fully readable, forkable and useful to
individuals while leaving the commercial rights intact.

PolyForm Noncommercial is a standard, lawyer-drafted licence from the
[PolyForm Project](https://polyformproject.org), not a bespoke one. Note that it
is *source-available*, not OSI open source — the noncommercial restriction is
exactly what disqualifies it, and that restriction is the point.
